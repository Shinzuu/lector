Study by listening. Ask when you get lost.

Lector is a free Chrome extension that reads any web page or PDF aloud in a natural voice, explains slides the way a tutor would, and answers your questions out loud when you pause it.

Download for Chrome Set up in 3 minutes

Free. Works with Chrome, Edge and Brave on Windows, Mac and Linux.

Made for revising, not just reading

Text-to-speech usually reads every page number and footer in a flat voice. Lector cleans the page up first and talks like a person.

Slides and PDFs, explained

In Narrated mode each slide becomes a short spoken explanation: bullet points turn into sentences, formulas are said in words, and headers, footers and page numbers are left out.

Pause and ask

Press A and ask out loud, like "why is it like this?". Lector answers from the page you are on, reads the answer to you, then carries on when you say "continue".

Skip what you don't need

See every slide or heading with how long it takes. Untick the boring ones, jump to any part, and watch the time left go down.

A voice you can listen to for hours

Natural neural voices, captions for every line, the current sentence highlighted, and speed from 0.8× to 2×.

Set up in 3 minutes

Lector isn't in the Chrome Web Store, so you add it yourself. It's a one-time thing.

  1. Download and unzip

    Click Download for Chrome, then unzip lector.zip somewhere you'll keep it, like your Documents folder. Don't delete the folder afterwards: Chrome runs Lector from it.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions in Chrome (type it in the address bar) and switch on Developer mode in the top right corner.

  3. Load Lector

    Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped lector folder (the one with manifest.json inside). Then click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Lector.

  4. Get a free Gemini key

    Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with Google, click Create API key, and copy it. This powers Narrated mode and questions. Reading aloud works without it.

  5. Paste the key

    Right-click the Lector icon, choose Options, paste the key under Gemini API key, and click Save. Press Test voice to pick the voice you like.

  6. For PDFs on your computer

    Back in chrome://extensions, click Details on Lector and turn on Allow access to file URLs. Or use the Open PDF button in Lector's viewer.

How to use it

Open a page or PDF, press the Lector icon (or Alt+Shift+R), and listen. It starts from whatever is on screen.

Space Play / pausePick up exactly where you stopped.
A Ask a questionSpeak or type. Say "continue" to go on.
S SectionsSkip parts, jump around, see time left.
Shift+→ Next sectionNext slide or next heading.
← → SentenceRepeat the last line, or skip one.
M Narrated / OriginalTutor-style explanation, or the text as written.

Tip: after a chapter, press A and say "quiz me on this". Answering out loud makes it stick far better than listening again.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Lector is free and open source. The Gemini key is free too; Google's free tier is plenty for studying. On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its models, so don't use it on anything private.

What gets sent where?

Your key stays in your browser. The text being read goes to Microsoft's voice service to turn it into speech. In Narrated mode, and when you ask a question, the nearby text goes to Gemini (or Claude, if you use an Anthropic key). Spoken questions are turned into text by Chrome's speech recognition, which uses Google. Nothing goes to us; there is no server.

The voice sounds robotic

Open Options and check that Neural (Microsoft, online) is selected and pick a voice with Test voice. If the bar ever says "using Chrome's voice", the online voice couldn't be reached; it comes back when you reload the page.

It mispronounces a word

Add it in Options under Pronunciation fixes, one per line, like Kafka = Kaf-ka.

Nothing happens when I click the icon

Chrome doesn't let extensions run on its own pages (chrome://, the Web Store) or on its built-in PDF viewer. For a PDF, click the icon and it reopens in Lector's viewer.

How do I update?

Download the new zip, replace the old folder's contents, and click the reload arrow on Lector in chrome://extensions. Your settings stay.
