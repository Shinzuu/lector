Lector settings

Reads pages and PDFs aloud. Pause any time to ask about what you just heard.

Question answering

Free from aistudio.google.com/apikey. On the free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its models.

From console.anthropic.com, billed per use. A Claude Code or claude.ai login does not work here.

Keys are stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and sent only to the provider you pick.

Reading

Narrated mode has the model rewrite each slide or section as a short spoken explanation: headers, footers and names dropped, bullets turned into sentences, terms written the way they are said. It needs the key above, uses about one request per four pages, and is cached so re-listening is free. Original mode reads the text as written, with repeated headers and footers removed. Switch any time with M or the chip on the bar.

Applies in both modes and to answers. Built-in fixes already cover YARN, HDFS, ORC, Parquet, JSON, e.g., vs, units like 10 TB, and arrows.

Voice

Near-human voices from the service behind Edge's Read Aloud. Free and needs no account or Edge browser, but it is unofficial, so if it ever stops working Lector switches to Chrome's voices.

Keys

Alt+Shift+RStart or stop reading (or click the toolbar icon)
Alt+Shift+A or APause and ask. Speak, or type. Then say “continue”.
SpacePlay / pause
← →Previous / next sentence
Shift+← Shift+→Previous / next section (slide or heading)
SSections list: untick to skip, click to jump
MSwitch Narrated / Original
EscClose the question panel, or stop

Select text and right-click “Lector: read from here” to start at that point. Change shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
